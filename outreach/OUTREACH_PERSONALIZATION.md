# Outreach Personalization Rules

> **Purpose:** Preserve the relationship-based personalization that should be used in acquisition sourcing emails. Each recipient should receive a genuinely customized note, not a mail-merge email.
>
> Last updated: **2026-08-24**

---

## Core Rule

Every recipient email should be written individually around:

1. **One genuine personal or institutional connection** to Ron, if one exists.
2. **One specific reason the recipient is professionally relevant** to the acquisition search.
3. A concise explanation that Ron is seeking **one established Canadian branded business to personally own and grow**.
4. A low-pressure ask: keep Ron in mind when an appropriate owner begins thinking about succession, retirement, liquidity, tariffs, or another temporary challenge.
5. Never ask the recipient to breach client confidentiality.
6. Attach the bilingual buyer profile where appropriate.
7. **Every acquisition outreach email must be signed exactly: `Ron Shlien, MSM`.** This is a locked signature rule. Do not shorten it to `Ron`, `Ron Shlien`, or any other variation.

The personalization should feel natural and understated. Do not stack credentials or force a connection when a more personal one exists.

---

## Personalization Priority Order

When more than one connection exists, use this order:

1. Genuine personal/shared-community relationship
2. Prior direct professional relationship
3. Shared institutional recognition or award
4. Specific relevance of the recipient's practice / client base
5. Generic firm affiliation only as a last resort

The strongest emails should feel like **Ron writing to one person**, not a campaign.

---

## Tone

- Personal
- Warm
- Serious
- Concise
- Founder-to-founder / operator-to-adviser
- Never salesy
- Never boastful
- Never mass-email language
- Credentials should support trust, not dominate the note
