## 2. Selection Criteria

Prioritize people who are likely to have long-standing relationships with founder-owned or family-owned private companies fitting one or both acquisition triggers:

1. **Succession / retirement** — later-career founder or owner beginning to think about liquidity, succession or a gradual transition.
2. **Temporary dislocation** — fundamentally strong branded company facing tariff-related or other fixable earnings pressure.

Best-fit businesses remain established Canadian branded companies, ideally 25+ years old, generally $15M-$50M revenue and $3M-$6M normalized EBITDA, with spirits/beverage, food/CPG, sporting/outdoor or other branded specialty products favored.

## 5. Outreach Status Definitions

- **Researching** — fit is still being researched.
- **Identified** — name and role selected.
- **Email verified** — exact direct business email confirmed from a reliable source.
- **Ready to send** — contact and email verified; outreach prepared.
- **Sent** — initial outreach actually sent.
- **Follow-up due** — no response and follow-up is due.
- **Replied** — target responded.
- **Call scheduled** — conversation booked.
- **Met / spoke** — substantive discussion completed.
- **Intro promised** — advisor indicated they may introduce an owner or colleague.
- **Intro received** — actual owner/company introduction received.
- **Not a fit** — contact does not serve the right client base.
- **Parked** — worth retaining but not currently actionable.

## 6. Outreach Discipline

For every person contacted, record:

1. Date of initial outreach
2. Channel
3. Exact verified email
4. Whether the buyer one-pager was attached
5. Personalization used
6. Response or non-response
7. Follow-up date
8. Any call or meeting date
9. Specific client sectors they appear to cover
10. Any owner/company introduction received
11. Next action

No outreach should live only in memory or email.
