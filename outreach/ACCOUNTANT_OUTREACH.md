## 2. Who We Want to Reach

The highest-value accountant is not simply a senior partner. The target is someone with long-standing relationships with **founder-owned and family-owned private companies** that may fit one or both acquisition triggers:

1. **Succession / retirement:** a later-career founder or long-time owner beginning to think about retirement, succession, liquidity, or a gradual transition.
2. **Temporary dislocation:** a fundamentally strong branded company facing tariff-related or another fixable earnings/valuation pressure.

The strongest referral combines both.

### Priority roles

1. Private Enterprise / Private Company partner
2. Audit partner serving owner-managed mid-market businesses
3. Tax partner with long-standing founder/family relationships
4. Corporate Finance / M&A partner
5. Transaction Advisory partner
6. Restructuring / Turnaround partner
7. Family Business / Succession Planning leader

### Priority client profile

- Established Canadian branded business
- Ideally 25+ years old
- Revenue generally $15M-$50M
- Sustainable / normalized EBITDA generally $3M-$6M
- Spirits & beverage, food/CPG, sporting/outdoor goods, or other branded specialty products
- Quebec preferred; Ontario secondary
- Founder or long-time owner later in career
- No obvious family or management successor is especially attractive
- Owner does not need to leave immediately and may support an orderly transition
- Seller may retain a minority interest if mutually attractive, but rollover is not required
- Meaningful U.S. exposure can be attractive where tariffs have temporarily impaired earnings or value

## 3. Firm Priorities

### Tier 1 — Highest priority

- **KPMG** — explicitly prioritized; focus on Quebec/Ontario private enterprise, tax, deal advisory, and restructuring partners.
- Other Big Four firms with substantial private-company practices in Quebec and Ontario.
- Leading Canadian and regional firms with deep founder/family-business relationships.

### Selection principle

**Quality over quantity.** A partner with 25 long-term founder clients is more valuable than a senior title with little direct exposure to owner-managed businesses.

Do not add a person simply to reach 50 names. Each target should have a clear reason they are likely to know owners matching the acquisition thesis.

## 5. Outreach Status Definitions

Use these status labels consistently:

- **Researching** — person or fit is still being researched.
- **Identified** — name and role selected as a target.
- **Email verified** — direct business email confirmed from a reliable source.
- **Ready to send** — contact verified and outreach prepared.
- **Sent** — initial outreach actually sent.
- **Follow-up due** — no response and follow-up is due.
- **Replied** — target responded.
- **Call scheduled** — conversation booked.
- **Met / spoke** — substantive discussion completed.
- **Intro promised** — accountant indicated they may introduce an owner or colleague.
- **Intro received** — actual owner/company introduction received.
- **Not a fit** — contact does not serve the right client base.
- **Parked** — worth retaining but not currently actionable.

## 6. Outreach Objective

The goal is **not** to ask accountants to disclose confidential client information. The goal is to make the acquisition profile memorable enough that, when an appropriate owner raises retirement, succession, tariffs, fatigue, or a possible sale, the accountant thinks of Ron as a credible principal buyer.

Core referral question:

> **Which long-time business owner in your client base is approaching retirement, has no obvious successor, or is being worn down by tariffs or another temporary challenge — and may quietly be thinking about what happens next?**

The actual outreach should emphasize:

- Principal buyer, not broker
- One high-quality acquisition, not a volume strategy
- Respect for owner legacy, employees, and brand
- No need for seller to leave immediately
- Optional seller minority rollover
- Ability to engage confidentially before a formal process
- Interest in both succession situations and temporarily dislocated businesses

## 7. Follow-up Discipline

For every person contacted, record:

1. Date of initial outreach
2. Channel: email / LinkedIn / phone / introduction
3. Whether the buyer one-pager was attached or linked
4. Response or non-response
5. Follow-up date
6. Any call or meeting date
7. Specific client sectors they appear to cover
8. Any owner/company introduction received
9. Whether permission was given to stay in touch
10. Next action

No outreach should live only in memory or email. Once it happens, update this tracker.
